Arrowlith — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-17

Arrowlith is an offline puzzle game by Eidvik Studio (Denis Eidukevicius, individual developer, Lithuania). The game does not collect, send, share or sell any personal data. The only optional exception is Google Play Games, described below.

No account, no internet

The game needs no account and does not send any data over the internet. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. The only exception is the optional Google Play Games connection described below.

Data on your device

Your progress and settings (levels, stars, achievements, language, sound) are saved only in the game's private storage on your device. The developer has no access to them.

Google Play Games

If you use Google Play Games on your device, the game can sign you in with your Play Games profile. Your unlocked achievements and records (best endless run, stars collected) are then sent to Google Play Games and linked to your profile. Leaderboards show your gamer name and avatar to other players according to your Play Games profile visibility settings. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The developer sees only leaderboard entries and summary statistics in Google Play Console and does not receive your email address or other contact details. You can play without Google Play Games.

Android backup

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the game's save file in your own Google account backup, so your progress can move to a new phone. You and Google control this backup; the developer cannot see it.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders locally on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Rating window

The game may show the standard Google Play rating window. It is run by Google Play; the game does not receive your rating or review.

Purchases

The game currently has no purchases. If optional paid content is added later, payment will be processed only by Google Play; the developer will not receive your payment details, and this policy will be updated.

Children

The developer does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Deleting data

Uninstalling the game deletes all of its data from your device. You can also clear it in Android settings (Apps > Arrowlith > Storage > Clear data). Google Play Games data for this game can be deleted in your Play Games profile settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date and shown in the game.

Contact
